# NORTHSTAR — Apps

What each repository is *for*. The code is the authority on what exists; this is the authority on what it is meant to become.

NORTHSTAR's software exists to run one loop: **qualitative context about a business, plus numbers trustworthy enough to bet on, produce a prioritized judgment about what to do next — the work is divided between NORTHSTAR and the client, executed, and then reviewed against results before the next turn.** Each repository owns one part of that loop, and nothing belongs in any of them that doesn't serve it.

## northstar-api

The spine, and the only permanent one. Everything that has to be *right* lives here: what each KPI means, the math that produces it, the shape of a report, and the contract between a business's data and the intelligence that interprets it. That colocation is deliberate — the methodology is the product, its pieces change together, and no boundary is worth putting between them. Its mission is a single trustworthy account of a client's business, where every number reconciles against the system it came from; a wrong number in front of a client is the only real emergency, and this is the repo whose job is to prevent one. Integrations exist to serve that account and point inward: data flows in so the business can be understood, and the system does not reach back into a client's platforms to change what they sell or what they charge. Definitions precede adapters, always — what a number means is a one-way door, how it is fetched is not. Over years, what compounds here is the aggregate view across every client NORTHSTAR has ever served: a picture of LatAm commerce that can only be earned by running the business, and that makes every recommendation sharper than an outsider's guess.

## northstar-web

The client's application, and the long-term center of gravity of the whole product. Its mission is to be the Growth OS a client actually operates — where a founder or a commercial lead goes to understand where the business stands and, above everything else, to know what to do next. It is not an analytics tool and must never decay into a metrics browser. The cycle is its permanent metronome, and the periodic report is the heartbeat: a real diagnosis, a small number of high-impact priorities, and a clear division of who does what. Optimize for depth and direction per visit; frequency of visits is not the goal and never becomes one. Over time this app takes ownership of the qualitative brain of the business — ideal customer, positioning, market context, catalog reality — held and maintained by the client, because a business's understanding of itself belongs to the business. It likewise absorbs whatever parts of onboarding and the commercial relationship stop needing a human between them. What it never absorbs is execution: NORTHSTAR does the commercial work inside the client's own platforms, and this app is where that work is decided, tracked, and reviewed — not where it is performed. A conversational agent may eventually become a reason to return more often; it would sit on top of the cycle, not replace it.

## northstar-console

The staff application. Its mission is to make an entire book of clients legible to a very small team, because leverage is the whole business model: a handful of people delivering C-level growth work across dozens of companies at once only works if the state of every client is visible at a glance. Its long-term shape is an instrument panel rather than a control panel — client health, trustworthiness of data, delivery status, and NORTHSTAR's own economics. Anything here that acts *upon* a client exists only because that action cannot yet be handled by the client themselves; those capabilities are meant to thin out as the client's app takes over more of the relationship. What remains is observation, plus the irreducibly human work that should never be automated away. It stays a separate application from the client's, permanently: two audiences, two deploys, and staff-facing code should never ship in the same build as client-facing code — a boundary that holds no matter how narrow the console becomes.

## northstar-foundation

Not an application. It is the context spine: the slow-changing truth about who NORTHSTAR serves, what it is for, how work gets decided, and which choices were expensive enough to deserve a written record. Its mission is that any mind arriving at this system — mine or an agent's — starts with the right frame instead of reverse-engineering one from source code, because the bottleneck here is never writing the code, it is knowing what to build. Its discipline is the inverse of every other repo: it describes the outside world and past reasoning, never the system's own structure, since the code is always the more accurate account of itself. Contracts live where they can be enforced; what lives here is why they exist. This is permanent by design — implementations are expected to be thrown away, and the frame they were built inside is what survives them.

## Invariants

- **The moat** is trust and demonstrated revenue lift, plus the structured qualitative brain held per client and the framework that turns numbers into the right priorities. Nothing should be optimized that doesn't compound into one of those.
- **The AI ambition is bounded.** Agents replace the analyst — the diagnosis, the prioritization, the reasoning. They do not replace the operator who executes, and they do not replace the relationship.
- **Never:** native mobile applications; any public API, marketplace, or third-party developer surface; automated write-back into a client's platforms.
- **Direction of travel:** the client owns more over time — their context, their access, more of the relationship's mechanics. The staff surface narrows toward observation; the client surface widens toward ownership.
